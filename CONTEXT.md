# egouda.xyz

Personal website context for Essam Gouda's public portfolio, blog, and professional services.

## Language

**Agents Consultant**:
A professional who designs and sets up agentic workflows for another team.
_Avoid_: Chatbot builder, generic AI consultant

**Agentic Pipeline**:
A business workflow where AI agents use tools, context, and review points to complete operational work.
_Avoid_: Chatbot, script, automation

**Services Lead**:
A person or organization that contacts Essam about a potential consulting engagement.
_Avoid_: User, customer, account

**Agent Brief Prompt**:
A provider-tuned system prompt that a **Services Lead** copies into their preferred AI agent so it can interview them and produce a Markdown brief.
_Avoid_: Embedded AI form, chatbot widget

**Agentic Pipeline Brief**:
A Markdown artifact generated by a **Services Lead**'s AI agent and emailed to Essam to start consulting discovery.
_Avoid_: Intake form, raw chat transcript

**Free Design POC**:
An unpaid proof-of-concept design for a candidate **Agentic Pipeline**. It proves whether the pipeline is worth building, but is not a free production build.
_Avoid_: Free implementation, trial subscription

**Engagement**:
An agreed consulting project with a scoped agentic pipeline outcome.
_Avoid_: Gig, task, ticket

**Email Forwarding Address**:
A public contact address that forwards inbound mail to a private inbox.
_Avoid_: Mailbox

## Relationships

- An **Agents Consultant** designs and sets up one or more **Agentic Pipelines** for a **Services Lead**.
- A **Services Lead** uses an **Agent Brief Prompt** with their own AI agent to create an **Agentic Pipeline Brief**.
- An **Agentic Pipeline Brief** can lead to a **Free Design POC**.
- A **Free Design POC** may become an **Engagement** if the **Services Lead** wants Essam to help build the pipeline.
- A **Services Lead** may become an **Engagement** after scope and fit are agreed.
- An **Email Forwarding Address** receives public inquiries before they become **Engagements**.

## Example dialogue

> **Dev:** "Should the services page promise a chatbot?"
> **Domain expert:** "No. Position it as an **Agentic Pipeline**: tools, data, evaluation, and handoff around a real workflow."

## Flagged ambiguities

- "Mailbox" was used for `me@egouda.xyz`; resolved as **Email Forwarding Address**, because inbound mail forwards to a private inbox rather than living in a standalone mailbox.
